ℹ About the Two Determinant Counts

Combinatorial Count: Total number of ways to distribute α and β electrons independently: C(n,n_α) × C(n,n_β). This is the mathematical maximum of possible Slater determinant products.

GUGA-adapted Count: Approximate count of distinct spin-coupled determinants used in actual GUGA-based CI calculations (as in OpenMolcas, GAMESS). Each CSF is a linear combination of determinants with specific Clebsch-Gordan coefficients.

Relationship: For singlets, Det ≈ 2×CSF + √CSF. The exact expansion depends on the genealogical spin coupling scheme. Programs like OpenMolcas work primarily with the compact CSF basis.

References: Shavitt (1977, 1981) on GUGA formalism; Dobrautz et al. (2019) on spin-adapted FCIQMC

Standard RASSCF Notation

RAS(n, l, m; i, j, k)

This notation, established in the literature, specifies the RASSCF active space as follows:

  • n = number of active electrons
  • l = maximum number of holes allowed in RAS1
  • m = maximum number of electrons (excitations) in RAS3
  • i = number of orbitals in RAS1
  • j = number of orbitals in RAS2
  • k = number of orbitals in RAS3
Example: RAS(14, 2, 2; 4, 6, 4) means 14 active electrons, with up to 2 holes in RAS1 (4 orbitals), up to 2 excitations in RAS3 (4 orbitals), and 6 orbitals in RAS2.

Reference: Sauri, V.; Serrano-Andrés, L.; Shahi, A. R. M.; et al. "Multiconfigurational Second-Order Perturbation Theory Restricted Active Space (RASPT2) Method for Electronic Excited States: A Benchmark Study." J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2011, 7, 153–168. DOI: 10.1021/ct100478d
